The $100 Corner Office

Published 2026-05-17

This guide will help you set up a dedicated, functional workspace in an unused corner of your home for around $100 or less. This is perfect for remote work, studying, or side projects when space and budget are tight.

### Find Your Corner

Look for a corner that isn't actively used. This could be in a living room, bedroom, or even a wide hallway. Ideally, it's away from heavy foot traffic and has access to a power outlet. Measure the space to know your maximum desk dimensions.

### The Desk: Prioritize Function

Your desk doesn't need to be fancy. The goal is a flat surface for your laptop or monitor and enough space for a mouse.

**Look for:**

* **Small writing desks:** Check IKEA (Linnmon/Lagkapten tabletop with Adils legs are very affordable), Walmart, Target, or Amazon basics. These can often be found for $30-$50.

* **Used furniture:** Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, or local thrift stores are goldmines. You might find a sturdy, small desk for $10-$30. Don't be afraid of minor cosmetic flaws.

* **DIY shelf desk:** A sturdy shelf board mounted to the wall with brackets can serve as a floating desk. This saves floor space and can be very cheap. Lumber scraps or inexpensive shelving at hardware stores work.

**Budget Tip:** If your corner is tight, consider a corner desk unit to maximize surface area, but these are often pricier. A small rectangular desk pushed into the corner works perfectly fine. Aim for under $50 here.

### The Chair: Comfort Matters Most

You'll be spending hours here, so prioritize basic comfort. You don't need an expensive ergonomic chair, just something that won't give you back pain immediately.

**Look for:**

* **Kitchen/Dining Chair:** If you have an unused kitchen chair, try it first. Add a cushion for extra comfort.

* **Used office chairs:** Again, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, and thrift stores are your friends. People often sell basic office chairs for $20-$40. Look for something with decent back support and a height adjustment if possible.

* **New budget chairs:** Walmart or Amazon sometimes have basic task chairs for $40-$60. These are usually plastic but offer more comfort than a dining chair.

**Budget Tip:** Your existing dining chair can be a zero-cost start. Add a cheap seat cushion ($5-$10) if needed. Aim for under $50 here.

### Basic Lighting

Good lighting prevents eye strain. Your corner might be dim.

**Look for:**

* **Desk lamp:** A simple clip-on lamp or a small LED desk lamp from IKEA, Walmart, or Amazon can cost $10-$20. Position it to illuminate your workspace without creating glare on your screen.

* **Existing light:** If there's an overhead light nearby, make sure it's bright enough.

### Simple Organization

Clutter can be distracting. Keep your desk clear.

**Look for:**

* **Desk organizer:** A small pen holder and a spot for notes can be found for $5-$10 at dollar stores or discount retailers.

* **Wall shelf (optional):** If you found a wall-mounted desk, or have extra wall space, a small floating shelf above your desk can hold essentials and keep your main surface clear. These are very cheap ($10-$20).

**Budget Tip:** Use an old mug for pens. Reuse small boxes for supplies.

### Keep it Clean and Clear

Once set up, maintain your corner. Keep only essential items on your desk. This helps you focus and makes your small space feel less cramped. Your "corner office" is a dedicated space for work, even if it's small. Treat it like one.
